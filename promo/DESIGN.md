# Computah Promo — Design

## Style Prompt

Dark, technical, calm-confident SaaS. The video is the Computah website in motion:
near-black canvas, soft teal accent glow, monospace wordmark voice for anything
"system" (badges, labels, code), humanist sans for statements. Restraint over
spectacle — medium energy, clean push transitions, one verdict-green moment.

## Colors

- `#0a0b0f` — background (canvas)
- `#14161d` — panel (cards, chips)
- `#242833` — border / hairlines
- `#5eead4` — accent (teal — wordmark, highlights, glow)
- `#e7e9ee` — foreground text
- `#8a90a0` — muted text
- `#34d399` — pass green (verdict moment only)

## Typography

- **Geist** — statements & headlines (300 vs 800 weight contrast)
- **Geist Mono** — system voice: wordmark, badges, labels, code

## What NOT to Do

- No full-screen linear gradients (banding) — radial glow only
- No new colors outside the site palette
- No exit animations before transitions (transition IS the exit)
- No more than one verdict-green element on screen at a time
- No body text under 20px / labels under 16px
